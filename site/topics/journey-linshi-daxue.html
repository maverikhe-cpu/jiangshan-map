长沙临时大学西迁昆明 · 逐站时空坐标、古今地名与史料诗词详解（4 站）

第 1 站 天津（今天津）· 南开校舍被炸 Tianjin (Tianjin) — Nankai's campus is bombed
1937年（民国二十六年七月） · 在地图定位此站 →

1937年7月29日晨至30日下午，日军飞机轰炸南开大学，秀山堂、木斋图书馆、芝琴楼女生宿舍等被毁。坐标是现今天津城区，不是校园测绘点。

From the morning of 29 July to the afternoon of 30 July 1937, Japanese aircraft bombed Nankai University, destroying Xiushan Hall, the Muzhai Library, and the Zhiqin women's dormitory. The pin is today's Tianjin, not a surveyed campus point.

南开大学档案馆记述：1937年7月29日晨至30日下午，日军轰炸南开大学。Nankai University Archives: Japanese aircraft bombed Nankai from the morning of 29 July 1937 through the afternoon of the 30th.
第 2 站 长沙（今湖南长沙）· 长沙临时大学开学 Changsha (Changsha, Hunan) — Changsha Temporary University opens
1937年（民国二十六年十一月） · 在地图定位此站 →

1937年8月14日教育部密电，拟在长沙设临时大学。8月28日指定张伯苓、梅贻琦、蒋梦麟为筹备委员会常务委员，杨振声为秘书主任。长沙校舍在韭菜园圣经学校，男生宿舍在四十九标，女生宿舍在涵德女校。10月25日开学典礼，11月1日上课。文学院设于南岳圣经学校，见于11月17日筹备委员会工作报告，搬入日期待核，本图不单列南岳点。坐标是现长沙。

On 14 August 1937 the Ministry of Education cabled a plan for a temporary university in Changsha. On 28 August it named Zhang Boling, Mei Yiqi, and Jiang Menglin standing members of the preparatory committee, with Yang Zhensheng as secretary-general. In Changsha the offices and classrooms were at the Bible School in Jiucaiyuan, the men's dormitory at the 49th Regiment barracks, and the women's dormitory at Hande School. The opening ceremony was 25 October and classes began 1 November. The College of Arts was at the Nanyue Bible School in the 17 November report; the move-in date is unverified, so this map adds no Nanyue pin. The coordinate is today's Changsha.

清华大学校史馆引1937年11月17日筹备委员会工作报告：长沙部分校舍在韭菜园圣经学校，文学院在南岳圣经学校。Tsinghua University History Museum, citing the 17 November 1937 preparatory report: the Changsha buildings were at the Jiucaiyuan Bible School, and the College of Arts was at the Nanyue Bible School.
第 3 站 长沙（今湖南长沙）· 决定迁昆明 Changsha (Changsha, Hunan) — The decision to move to Kunming
1938年（民国二十七年一月） · 在地图定位此站 →

1938年1月22日布告，商呈教育当局迁往昆明。2月4日发布步行计划。学校核准878名学生随迁；284名学生领甲种许可证参加湘黔滇旅行团。南开大学档案馆作旅行团约300人、教师11人（黄钰生任指导委员会主席，同行有侯洛荀），人数待核。出发日待核：清华校友总会记述2月20日出发，清华大学校史馆引2月17日《大公报》称二十日前后启程。2月18日《大公报》称第二组六十余人乘粤汉车经香港入滇。海路（香港、海防、滇越铁路）与湘桂公路一路不在本图连线，逐站日期不足。站点之间的折线只是城市示意，不是实测路线，也不是全体走的同一条路。

A notice on 22 January 1938 said the school had asked to move to Kunming. A walking plan was posted on 4 February. The school licensed 878 students to move; 284 received the permit for the Hunan–Guizhou–Yunnan walking group. Nankai's archives say about 300 walkers and 11 teachers, with Huang Yusheng chairing the guidance committee and Hou Luoxun among them, so the headcount is unverified. The departure day is unverified: the Tsinghua alumni account says 20 February, while the Tsinghua history museum quotes the 17 February Dagongbao as saying they would leave around the 20th. The 18 February Dagongbao said a second party of sixty-odd people had taken the Canton–Hankou train through Hong Kong into Yunnan. The sea route (Hong Kong, Haiphong, the Yunnan–Vietnam railway) and the Hunan–Guangxi road are not drawn; stop-by-stop dates are lacking. Lines between pins are a city sketch, not a surveyed route and not one path taken by everyone.

清华大学校史馆引1938年1月22日迁校布告：本校商呈教育当局迁往昆明。出发日各记载不一致，待核。Tsinghua University History Museum, citing the 22 January 1938 notice: the school had asked the education authorities to move to Kunming. Published departure days disagree and are unverified.
第 4 站 昆明（今云南昆明）· 改称西南联大并上课 Kunming (Kunming, Yunnan) — Renamed, and classes begin in Kunming
1938年（民国二十七年五月） · 在地图定位此站 →

1938年4月2日教育部电令，国立长沙临时大学改称国立西南联合大学。清华校友总会记述旅行团4月19日经胜境关入云南平彝（今富源）、4月27日至大板桥、4月28日抵昆明，历时68天、约3500华里；南开大学档案馆作3000余里。里数待核，本图不把平彝、大板桥画成站点。1938年5月4日在昆明上课。坐标是现昆明。

On 2 April 1938 the Ministry of Education ordered National Changsha Temporary University renamed National Southwest Associated University. The Tsinghua alumni account says the walking group entered Yunnan at Shengjing Pass on 19 April, reached Pingyi (today's Fuyuan), was at Dabanqiao on 27 April, and entered Kunming on 28 April, after 68 days and about 3,500 li. Nankai's archives say more than 3,000 li. The distance is unverified, and this map does not add Pingyi or Dabanqiao as stops. Classes began in Kunming on 4 May 1938. The coordinate is today's Kunming.

北京大学档案馆与南开大学档案馆均记1938年4月2日改称国立西南联合大学。昆明上课日据南开新闻网与云南师范大学西南联大博物馆，为5月4日。The Peking University Archives and the Nankai University Archives both date the rename to 2 April 1938. Nankai News and the Southwest Associated University Museum at Yunnan Normal University date the Kunming opening of classes to 4 May.
